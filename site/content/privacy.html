Local sessions and settings

Gyro stores sessions, settings, logs, and app state on your Mac. When available, provider credentials live in macOS Keychain. No Gyro account is required for the current alpha.

What model providers receive

Providers receive the prompts, selected files, tool context, and anything else needed to complete your request. Their policies apply. Gyro does not proxy this traffic.

Telemetry and this website

App telemetry is off by default. This site has no analytics, ads, account gate, remote fonts, or tracking pixels. Cloudflare hosts the site and GitHub hosts downloads; both process normal request metadata under their own policies.

Delete your data

Delete sessions in Gyro. To delete the app, move Gyro.app to Trash. To remove local data, delete ~/Library/Application Support/Gyro. Provider data is managed by that provider.
